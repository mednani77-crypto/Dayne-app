DeynBook Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 September 2026
Version: 1.0

DeynBook is an offline debt-recording application for individuals and small businesses. Ledger data is designed to stay on the user's Android device.

Data handled by the app

Depending on what the user enters, DeynBook can store locally a business or ledger name, customer and supplier names, optional phone numbers and notes, debt and payment amounts, currencies, dates, descriptions, and app preferences.

Data collection and transmission

DeynBook 1.0 does not operate an application server and does not transmit ledger data to the developer. It does not use advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, cloud databases, Firebase, remote AI services, or account sign-in.

The Android application does not request the INTERNET permission. Android cloud backup is disabled.

Files created by the user

DeynBook can create statement PDFs, summary images, CSV exports and JSON backup files. These files are generated locally and leave the app only when the user explicitly chooses a destination or shares them through Android's system UI.

Phone numbers

If a user stores a phone number and chooses the call action, DeynBook opens the Android dialer. DeynBook does not read the device contact list, call log or SMS messages.

Financial-services disclaimer

DeynBook is a bookkeeping and record-keeping tool. It is not a bank, lender, credit bureau, money-transfer provider or payment processor. It does not provide loans, determine credit scores, transfer money or guarantee collection of debts.

Data retention and deletion

Ledger data remains on the device until the user deletes records, deletes all app data, clears Android app storage, or uninstalls the app. User-created exported files remain wherever the user saved them and must be deleted separately if desired.

Children

DeynBook is a general bookkeeping utility and is not designed specifically for children.

Changes

If a future version introduces an online service or a third-party SDK that changes data practices, this policy and the Google Play Data Safety declaration must be updated before release.

Contact

Use the developer contact shown on the Google Play listing for privacy questions.
